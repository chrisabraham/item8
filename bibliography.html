bibliography & references

This page is the edition’s end notes: every source the edition stands on, gathered in one place. Each entry gives the source as it was consulted, says in a line or two what it establishes for this edition, and names the pages that cite it — so a reader can walk from any claim on the site back to its paper.

The editor’s stated aim for the whole edition, in his words: “To stitch this all together as best as possible, to be an historical resource, with the hypertext writing on top as cream on top.” The bibliography is where the stitching shows: the work and its witnesses first, then the period paper, the people’s own accounts, the scholarship and the press, the institutional records, and last the testimony and the archive on which the rest rests.

Scibe’s bibliography gathers the same village’s other half of the paper: scibe.org/bibliography.html
