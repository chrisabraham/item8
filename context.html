context

The work on this site was written in a world that has mostly vanished: online conferences reached by telnet, run on conferencing software, kept by hosts, inside companies and programs whose names now need explaining. These pages are the explanation — the wrapper around the work, as the index, the glossary, and the faq are its reference shelf. Every entry lists the sources it consulted; where the record is thin, or the editor’s memory is the source, the entry says so. Where the sister edition at scibe.org treats the same ground, the entries cross-link. The editor’s thesis for the layer, in his words: “Like any museum, the art is only part of what makes Item 8 and Scibe interesting.” Provenance, people, and machinery are part of the exhibit here, not footnotes to it.

The sources those entries consulted — and every other source this edition stands on — are gathered in one place: the bibliography, where each entry names what it establishes and the pages that cite it.
