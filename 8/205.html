response 8:205 — the 182nd of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:205) Katherine Blanke (KAT) 27-MAY-98 18:30

I have been forever boxed in by my classmates as the girl they met 3, 5, 8, 12 years ago, depending on who you talk to. Thinking about graduation and how my life is going to change and be my own has changed me just by thinking about it. I've become bolder, shedding my self-consciousness like a snakeskin, finally being who I really am. Finally freeing myself of my own box that I let them trap me in. @!KAT
