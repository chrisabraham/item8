phlegm. yellow sometimes, clear. usually the color of what i just drank. morning's oj, the cafe au lait. this is an irish story, it doesn't have a happy ending. my great grandma read tea leaves. she was blind. described to me as a jigging oracle, reading leaves before the dark and listening to 78s on the record player late unto her twilight. i have faced my deamons, i am sure that's more than many of you can say. we work along the same roads now, the low road, the high road, and I don't know if the point of the game is to exorcise but to cohabitate. been told the deamons are self. reading a book on seth. seth. seth.

The vortex, the complex. the tampex. being told how much the period can hurt, how the woman is hit up for $250/month while men are charged but $103. That's what my insurance charts say.

in saint petersburg, babuska would approach holly and beg her, child, to get off the ground, get off the cement child, the cold rock -- it kills the womb, it injurs the uterus.
