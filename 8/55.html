response 8:55 — the 53rd of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:55) Hope O'Keeffe 12-JAN-98 16:20

So hard to sit back and watch the Galoise Blond, the Marlboro Red and not attack with a water pistol. I remember writing about it, watching myself almost slipping into the haze of Camel filters and the wrong man: camel haiku

Smoke curls, like kisses, gather dangerously... Then, slowly, I exhale.

listen — Hope O'Keeffe, in the voice cast for this edition
