response 8:181 — the 169th of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:181) Ginny Little 22-APR-98 11:49

i ride in a lift to the top of the roof to sign my name on paper, soon a copper sunburst will cover? who will ever see it, and why does it matter? whoever takes this down a hundred years from now. i wonder if they will wonder about who i was, way back when. now.
