response 8:64 — the 59th of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:64) Katherine Blanke (KAT) 17-JAN-98 19:22

Arising from under the waves of deep sleep, I rose in the morning to an empty and cold house, dirty dishes and food in the kitchen and the living room in just as poor condition. Another night my sister had a friend over, I could see. Then I think for the hundredth time: I gotta get outta here. Arising from beneath the cool water, I splashed and held my breath inbetween moments...at a pool in January. Stress of exams over until thwaveades arrive at my mailbox...

@!KAT
