response 8:152 — the 142nd of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:152) Dirk Flinthart 17-MAR-98 17:37

Sometimes I have found myself wondering at the colours and the skills and the images upon our greybrick walls by the steel river klickitiklick. Considering Pompeii. And if the hammer falls tomorrow drowns our world in fire and ash and if they come and dig us up again with brushes and picks and cameras and if they find our walls and not our books will it be these poets who are remembered to stand for us all?

listen — Dirk Flinthart, in the voice cast for this edition
