response 8:80 — the 74th of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:80) Ginny Little 21-JAN-98 13:27

i can taste the salty remnants of sadness in my throat. i feel like crying sometimes and don't, other times blubber without always knowing why, it just comes, too easily, too swiftly, without warning...maybe just in response to a toilet paper commercial, for chrissakes, what's up with that? menopausal, yea, so they tell me...and that is too soon too. but all things in their time. so i just watch and listen and hope.

listen — Ginny Little, in the voice cast for this edition
