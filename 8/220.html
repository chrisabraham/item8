8:220) Red 01-JUN-98 22:56

He talks too much. About the future...OUR future...together. I cringe each time and tell him to knock it off. He insists he loves me. Thinks about me all the time. That's how he gets to sleep at night. He thinks of me...and NOT in a sexual way he assured me today. He knows it's love because the only time he can stop thinking about me is when he plays pinball..which isn't often and when the quarters run out, his mind wanders back to thoughts of my beauty and the way I smell when I'm lying in his arms.... (: RED :)
