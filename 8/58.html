response 8:58 — the 55th of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:58) Hope O'Keeffe 13-JAN-98 18:53

sex; touch; the weaving of love. Each to its place and time, and yet I would not dismiss any of them: numinous mornings intertwined dreams, and dreamers septisensual.

listen — Hope O'Keeffe, in the voice cast for this edition
