response 8:77 — the 71st of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:77) Phillip J. Rhoades 20-JAN-98 17:44

Every tear I've shed has watered my dreams, and they've soaked it up and grown. Soon they will flower and bear the fruit of reality. They have drank of my pains and fed on my struggles, and now they will give me a good harvest.
