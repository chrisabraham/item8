8:215) Sarah Neyaz 29-MAY-98 22:43

He said he loved me, then he fell in love with someone else. He said he'd be with me forever, then he was with someone else. I've learned that Nothing lasts forever, we all know hearts can change. Everytime, i 'm always the one who loses in the end, I always end up getting hurt no matter what. My hearts been broken, been squeezed so many times, that there's nothing left. I don't think i can love anyone anymore, I don't think I can ever be so close to anyone because i'm so afraid i'll lose them the minute I turn around. I choose to live this way, to live this lonely life of mine, cus anyways i'll always end up being alone.
