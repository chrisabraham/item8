response 8:46 — the 44th of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:46) Dirk Flinthart 08-JAN-98 23:01

Somedays turn so swift to yesterdays if you don't watch carefully, carefully. Hand in hand we watch brave deeds unfold before us, turning, turning in the gyre of quickstep 2/4 time. A step out of time and your place is lost, whirled away by another, swifter in the dance. Wait your turn. Never miss your mark.

listen — Dirk Flinthart, in the voice cast for this edition
