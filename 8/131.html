response 8:131 — the 124th of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:131) Ginny Little 09-FEB-98 10:46

lst year valentine's day, i met you in DC. remember? oh, and yes, it prompted impetuous kissing, to show the young ones how. laughter and dancing, and new friends and one turned 18 and what a party because it was all a coming together over food, story, and virtual becoming real. i remember it fondly. it didn't hurt. it was a moment of beginnings, a journeying come crossroads. and here we are, at the inn. fire warms, tea kettle sings, hearts join, pull up a chair.

listen — Ginny Little, in the voice cast for this edition
