response 8:155 — the 145th of 212 that survive. Forward and back walk every number, 8:0 to 8:240; the missing have pages of their own.

8:155) chris abraham 30-MAR-98 11:09

mark suggested i kiss her mouth. i asked him. "when i go to meet someone at the airport i always feel shy. I stand there and grab the bag. i fear rejection." "kiss her right away, hold her right away, break the ice, she feels as fearful." she was the last person off the plane, behind a troupe of ukulele players from o'ahu. here for a yuke jamboree in florida.

listen — chris abraham, in the voice cast for this edition
