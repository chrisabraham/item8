What began as one text, collaboratively written by some chums in a TMN-sponsored conference called artist, on an item numbered eight, is preserved here entire: every response, in the order the river ran, September 2, 1997 to August 31, 1998, when the cessation of the exercise — completely arbitrarily — was announced.

This site is a snapshot, not a revival. Nothing here is open for posting; the water stopped in 1998 and stays stopped. It has two faces, because the work had two lives: web, the house your editor built for it at coelaboration.com in 2000, and cmd, the amber screen where it was actually written and read, one response at a time, at a Caucus prompt. Flip the switch at the top. Both are the same snapshot.

Please hesitate not to read it as it was read: downstream, one response at a time. — your editor
